## The bet
Run the entire investor journey — registration, KYC, investing, portfolio, support —
**natively inside WhatsApp**. No app or website required to transact. Among the first
platforms in Brazil to do this end-to-end.

## Market
- Brazilian retail / middle market
- Ticket size: **R$1,000–R$10,000 per offer**
- Ambition: **millions of customers** → high-volume, low-ticket economics

## Two-sided platform
- **Demand (investors):** acquisition, WhatsApp product, KYC/suitability
- **Supply (funding):** sourcing and underwriting credit/debt offers from issuers
