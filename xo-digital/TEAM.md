# XO Digital — Team

## Current state
4 people, roles undefined, all still part-time with other jobs:
- 2 informally on **technology**
- 2 informally on **funding / credit**
- **No one full-time on the platform. No clear owner of CVM 88 compliance.**

## The core problem
A part-time, role-ambiguous founding team cannot launch and run a regulated platform
serving (eventually) millions of investors. The first job is not hiring more people —
it's **converting the committed founders to full-time, accountable, named roles**, and
filling the compliance gap. Headcount on top of ambiguity multiplies the ambiguity.

## Step 1 — Define the 4 founders

| Today | Proposed role | Owns |
|---|---|---|
| Tech 1 | **CTO / Head of Engineering** | WhatsApp platform architecture, API integrations, security, infra, uptime |
| Tech 2 | **Head of Product** | Conversational UX, onboarding/KYC flows, investor experience, roadmap |
| Funding 1 | **Head of Credit / Origination** | Sourcing issuers, underwriting offers, building the launch pipeline |
| Funding 2 | **Head of Compliance & Risk** | CVM 88 ownership: suitability, limits, disclosure, recordkeeping, reporting |

Notes:
- Founder-CEO accountability must sit explicitly with one person (likely you) — owning
  fundraising, regulator relationship, hiring, and overall strategy.
- The **Compliance & Risk** seat is the most urgent. If no founder can credibly own
  CVM 88 full-time, this becomes the **first external hire**, not a "later" hire.
- "Full-time" is the gating decision. Anyone who can't go full-time at launch should
  be moved to advisor/equity status, not left holding a critical seat part-time.

## Step 2 — First external hires (in priority order)
1. **Compliance Officer** *(if Funding 2 can't own it full-time)* — CVM 88 / crowdfunding
   or broker-dealer background. Non-negotiable before scaling.
2. **Growth / Marketing Lead** — owns Instagram, WhatsApp acquisition funnels, referral
   mechanics, content. Critical to the "millions of customers" thesis.
3. **Backend / Payments Engineer** — PIX, ledger accuracy, WhatsApp API at scale.
4. **Customer Operations Lead** — semi-automated WhatsApp support, escalations, trust.

## Step 3 — The founding story (for hiring & fundraising)
A strong, specific narrative attracts strong people. Anchor on:
- **Speed of execution:** founded and fully CVM 88-authorized in ~6 months.
- **A real wedge:** first to put the entire investing journey inside WhatsApp — not
  "another fintech app."
- **Built-in expertise:** founders already span technology and credit/funding.
- **A big, concrete market:** Brazilian retail investors, low tickets, mass scale.

Use this framing verbatim in job posts and outreach instead of generic "fintech
startup" language. Candidates join missions and stories, not feature lists.

- Who is **CEO of record** and owns the regulator relationship?
- Can Funding 2 truly own CVM 88 compliance, or do we hire that first?
