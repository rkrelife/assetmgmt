# XO Digital — Competitor Analysis

> Snapshot as of July 2026. Figures from public sources; verify before quoting. This
> is updated weekly by the competitor-watch agent (see `agent/` and `COMPETITOR_LOG.md`).

The three named competitors all operate under **CVM Resolution 88**, but each attacks a
different slice. None of them is WhatsApp-native mass-retail — that is XO's open lane.

## Quick comparison

| | **AmFi** ("Anthie") | **PeerBR** ("Perth Capital") | **Liqi** | **XO Digital (us)** |

| Core model | Tokenized credit / securitization on blockchain | Private-credit crowdfunding (receivables, precatórios) | RWA tokenization platform (issuer-side) | WhatsApp-native retail investing in credit notes |

## What each did RIGHT (learn from)
- **PeerBR** — captured ~60% of CVM emission share by *owning supply*: a strong issuer
  pipeline and origination via the GCB group's capital-markets network. Lesson: **supply
  relationships win this market.** Their growth (+371% YoY) came from deal flow, not ads.
- **AmFi** — built credibility as "first authorized to publicly offer tokenized credit,"
  used regulatory firsts as marketing, and landed big partnerships (Rayls, $1bn RWA
  pipeline). Lesson: **regulatory firsts + partnerships = trust and deal supply.**
- **Liqi** — went deep on the *issuer/infrastructure* side and rode the RWA-tokenization
  narrative to R$1.2bn cumulative. Lesson: **owning issuance infrastructure creates a
  supply moat** others depend on.

## What they got WRONG / left open (our opening)
- **All three skew institutional / sophisticated / higher-ticket.** None has cracked
  true *mass retail* with a frictionless consumer interface. Their onboarding is
  web/portal-based and still feels like a finance product.
- **None is WhatsApp-native.** The everyday Brazilian who lives in WhatsApp is not their
  target — that is precisely XO's wedge and low-ticket volume thesis.
- **Blockchain/tokenization framing** (AmFi, Liqi) adds a comprehension barrier for
  ordinary retail. XO can win by hiding all complexity behind a chat conversation.
- **Complexity as a moat cuts both ways** — it also caps their addressable audience.

## Strategic reads for XO
1. **Don't fight them on their turf** (institutional tokenization / big tickets). Win the
   segment they can't serve well: mass retail via WhatsApp, tiny tickets, huge numbers.
2. **But copy their supply discipline.** PeerBR/AmFi prove that whoever controls quality
   issuer flow wins. Our Chief Credit Officer must build that pipeline relentlessly.
3. **Use regulatory + product firsts as marketing** like AmFi did — "first fully
   WhatsApp-native CVM-88 platform already paying investors back."
4. **Watch the CVM 88 reform** (public consultation ran Sep–Dec 2025; overhaul on the
   2026 agenda, incl. securitization/tokenization). Rule changes could reshape every
   competitor's model — an area to monitor weekly.
